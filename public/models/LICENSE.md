# TrainTrack AI passenger-train illustration

The geometry and materials in `traintrack-passenger-train.glb` were procedurally
created for TrainTrack AI. They do not contain a downloaded model, texture, or
other third-party asset.

The model is dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode

The railway scene is illustrative and does not represent live train movement.
